Health_Monitor: Just three heart sprites that will disappear when the player takes damage - messy but functional, will certainly clean later. However, it's clear what it's meant to be, and it works to communicate what it needs to, so it will work for now

Skill_Icon: A sprite and text, just to show when the player can use a skill again. Text is TextMeshPro UI to work in tandem with the parent canvas object, meant to be debug until better visuals are in place

Minimap_Background: Plain white square to denote where the minimap will fit, to determine sizing to see if it interferes with playability. Not much more is helpful until room generation/transitions are added.
